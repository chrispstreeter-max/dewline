Do I need an app for the routine quiz?

No. The quiz is built from theme editor blocks: questions, answers and results. Create a page, assign the page.quiz template and edit it in the theme editor. See Routine quiz.

Why don't my key ingredients or supplement facts show on a product?

These blocks hide when they have no content. Check that:

  1. The metafield definition exists with the exact key, for example custom.key_ingredients.
  2. The product has a value for it in the Metafields card.
  3. The block is connected to that metafield in the theme editor.

See Product data with metafields.

My supplement facts table puts a nutrient in the wrong place. Why?

Nutrient lines need three parts: Nutrient | amount | % Daily Value. A line with only two parts is read as a serving line, like Serving size | 1 scoop. If a nutrient has no Daily Value, put † in the third part.

Can I use one product template for skincare and supplements?

Yes. Every data block hides itself when its metafield is empty, so a serum won't show supplement facts and a vitamin won't show a results timeline unless you fill them in.

How does the quiz pick a result?

Each answer adds points to one or more result keys, like dry:2, sensitive. The result with the most points wins. On a tie, the result higher in your block list wins. See the points format.

Can shoppers subscribe and save?

Yes. Install a subscriptions app such as Shopify Subscriptions and add your products to a plan. The Buy buttons block then shows one-time and subscription options with the discount and delivery frequency. No theme setting is needed.

Do routines and quiz results add subscriptions?

No. The routine block, the routine builder and quiz results add one-time purchases. Shoppers choose a subscription on the product page.

How do I change the free shipping amount?

Go to Theme settings > Cart > Free shipping threshold and enter an amount, like 50. Leave it empty to hide the bar. Remember to set the matching free shipping rate in Settings > Shipping and delivery.

Where do the collection filters come from?

From Shopify's free Search and Discovery app. Add filters there (price, availability, product type, tags or metafields like skin type) and they appear on collection and search pages.

How do I link to one ingredient in the glossary?

Add # and the ingredient name in lowercase with hyphens to the page URL, for example /pages/ingredients#hyaluronic-acid. See Ingredient glossary.

Can I switch between the Dewline, Pigment and Tonic styles?

Yes. You can add the theme again with another style, or change the style from Theme settings. Changing the style replaces your colors, fonts and layout settings, but not your section content. See Styles guide.

Is Dewline translated?

The theme's storefront text is included in English and French. For other languages, and to translate your own content, use Shopify's Translate and Adapt app. See Translations and markets.

Does Dewline show star ratings?

Yes, when you use a product reviews app that fills Shopify's product rating. Ratings show on product cards, in the Title block and in the Rating block.

Is Dewline accessible?

Dewline is built to WCAG 2.1 AA: visible focus, labels on every field, keyboard support for menus, tabs and the quiz, screen reader announcements for cart and quiz updates, and reduced motion support. Keep your own colors high contrast and add alt text to your images.

Can I show results claims like "94% saw brighter skin"?

The Results section, Results timeline block and hero badge are built for claims, but the numbers in the demo are examples. Only publish results you can support, and use the disclaimer or footnote setting to say where they come from.

How do I get help?

Use the support form. We reply within 2 business days. Include your store URL and a screenshot if you can.
