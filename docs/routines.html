Routines and subscriptions

Dewline helps shoppers buy a whole routine, not just one product. There are two places to build routines: the Routine block on product pages and the Routine builder section on the home page (or any page).

The product page routine block

The Routine block shows a morning and an evening routine with tabs, a checkbox for each step, a live total and an Add routine to bag button.

Set it up with metafields (recommended)

  1. Create the custom.routine_am and custom.routine_pm metafield definitions as List of products. See Product data with metafields.
  2. Open a product in your admin and pick its morning and evening products in the Metafields card, in the order they are used.
  3. Save. The default product template already connects the block to these metafields.

Settings

  • Heading and Text: shown above the routine, for example "Complete the routine" and "Uncheck a step you already have."
  • Morning > Products and Evening > Products: up to eight products each. Connect them to metafields so every product shows its own routine.
  • Step labels: optional words for each step, separated by commas, in order, for example Cleanse, Tone, Treat. Without labels, each step shows its product type.

How it behaves:

  • If only one routine has products, the tabs are hidden.
  • If neither has products, the block is hidden on that product (you will see a reminder in the theme editor).
  • Every available product starts checked. Shoppers uncheck what they already own and the total updates.
  • Sold-out products show as sold out and cannot be added.

Tip: step labels are set on the block, so they are the same on every product. If your routines vary a lot by product, leave the labels empty and set clear product types instead.

The home page routine builder

The Routine builder section shows a full routine with Morning and Evening tabs and a summary panel with the number of steps, the total and an Add routine to bag button.

  1. In the theme editor, open the home page and select Add section > Routine builder (the demo home page already includes one).
  2. Set the Eyebrow, Heading and Text.
  3. Under Summary, set the Heading and an optional Note, for example a free shipping message.
  4. Select each Step block and set:
    • Product
    • Step label, for example "Cleanse"
    • Time of day: Morning, Evening, or Morning and evening
  5. Add up to 12 steps. They show in block order and are numbered automatically within each tab.

Steps without a product show a placeholder so you can see where they go.

What gets added to the bag

Both routines add one of each checked, available product as a regular one-time purchase. Only the tab that is open is added: switch to Evening to add the evening routine.

Subscriptions (subscribe and save)

Dewline supports Shopify selling plans in the Buy buttons block. There is nothing to turn on in the theme.

  1. Install a subscriptions app, such as Shopify Subscriptions, from the Shopify App Store.
  2. In the app, create a subscription plan and add your products to it.
  3. Open a product page in your store. The Buy buttons block now shows Purchase options:
    • One-time purchase with its price
    • Subscribe and save with the subscription price, a "Save up to X%" badge when the plan has a discount, and a Delivery frequency menu
  4. When a shopper chooses Subscribe and save, the product price updates to the subscription price.

If a product can only be bought as a subscription, the one-time option is hidden. Products without plans show the normal buy button.

Note: the quiz, routine block and routine builder add one-time purchases. Shoppers choose a subscription on the product page.
