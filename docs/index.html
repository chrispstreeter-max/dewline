Set up Dewline, step by step

Plain-English guides for every part of the theme, from your first logo to quiz scoring and supplement facts.

  1. 01Getting startedSet up Dewline in about fifteen minutes: add the theme, pick a style, then set your logo, colors and fonts.
  2. 02Styles guideDewline comes with three styles. Each style sets the colors, fonts, corner radius and button shape. Sections, templates and your content are the same in every style, so you can pick the look that fits your brand and still use every feature.
  3. 03Product data with metafieldsDewline's product blocks (key ingredients, results, supplement facts, free from, how to use, the full ingredient list and routines) read their content from metafields. You create each metafield once, fill it in on every product, and each product page shows its own data.
  4. 04Routine quizThe routine quiz asks shoppers a few questions, one at a time, then recommends a routine with its products and an Add my routine to bag button. It is built entirely from theme editor blocks, so you do not need an app.
  5. 05Ingredient glossaryThe ingredient glossary is an A to Z page of every ingredient you use: what it does, its INCI name and which products contain it. Shoppers can search it, filter it by category and jump to a letter.
  6. 06Routines and subscriptionsDewline helps shoppers buy a whole routine, not just one product. There are two places to build routines: the Routine block on product pages and the Routine builder section on the home page (or any page).
  7. 07Product pagesThe product page is built from blocks inside the Product information section. Add, remove and drag blocks in the theme editor to set the order shoppers read them in.
  8. 08Home page sectionsBuild your home page from sections. In the theme editor, open the home page and select Add section. Drag sections to reorder them. Every content section has a Color scheme and Top padding and Bottom padding settings (0 to 120 px).
  9. 09Collections, search and cartThis page covers how shoppers browse and buy: collection pages, filters, search, the cart drawer, the free shipping bar and product recommendations.
  10. 10Translations and marketsDewline's storefront text comes in English and French. Every button, label and message is included in both languages, so a French storefront needs no extra work for the theme's own text.
