Product pages

The product page is built from blocks inside the Product information section. Add, remove and drag blocks in the theme editor to set the order shoppers read them in.

To edit it, go to Online Store > Themes > Customize and choose Products > Default product from the page selector at the top.

Section settings

Select Product information to set:

  • Gallery layout: Carousel (one image at a time with thumbnails) or Grid.
  • Image ratio: Adapt to image, Square or Portrait.
  • Color scheme, Top padding and Bottom padding.

The gallery supports images, videos, YouTube or Vimeo videos and 3D models.

Blocks

Block What it does
Title Product title, with optional vendor and star rating (from your reviews app). Choose the heading size.
Subtitle A short line under the title. Connect it to the Shopify subtitle metafield (descriptors.subtitle).
Price Price, compare-at price and unit price. Optional tax and shipping note.
Rating Star rating and review count from a product reviews app. Links to the reviews on the page.
Variant picker Buttons or a dropdown. Shows color swatches when your options have swatches set in the product's category metafields.
Buy buttons Quantity, add to cart, dynamic checkout buttons, subscriptions, pickup availability, Shop Pay installments, low stock note.
Icon row Up to four small icons with labels, for example Cruelty-free, Vegan, Dermatologist tested.
Description The product description from your admin.
Key ingredients Hero ingredients with what they do. See Product data.
Results timeline Results over time, like "4 weeks: 88% saw less redness".
Supplement facts An accessible supplement facts table.
Free from Badges for what the product does not contain.
Routine Morning and evening routines with "Add routine to bag". See Routines.
How to use Numbered steps, as a block or a collapsible row.
Ingredient list The full INCI list in a collapsible row.
Collapsible row Any text, for example shipping and returns. Can also show the content of a page.
Text, Heading, Eyebrow, Buttons General content blocks.
Concern links Links to collections (or a menu) as chips, for example "Dryness", "Redness".
Share A share button that uses the device's share menu, or copies the link.
Custom Liquid Your own Liquid code or an app snippet.

App blocks from your installed apps (reviews, size guides and so on) also appear in the Add block menu.

Buy buttons settings

  • Show quantity selector: also shows quantity rules (minimum, maximum and increments) when you set them in your admin for B2B or wholesale.
  • Show dynamic checkout buttons: shows the shopper's preferred payment method, like Shop Pay, Apple Pay or PayPal.
  • Show pickup availability: see below.
  • Low stock note threshold: shows "Only X left" when inventory is at or below this number. Set to 0 to hide it. Requires Shopify to track inventory for the product.

Pickup availability

If you offer local pickup, the Buy buttons block shows whether the selected variant is ready for pickup at your nearest location, and how soon. Shoppers can open Check pickup at other stores to see every location.

To turn it on:

  1. In your admin, go to Settings > Shipping and delivery and set up Local pickup for at least one location.
  2. In the theme editor, make sure Show pickup availability is on in the Buy buttons block.

Pickup only shows for products that require shipping and are stocked at a pickup location.

Shop Pay installments

When Shop Pay Installments is available for your store, the Buy buttons block shows the installments message (for example "Pay in 4 interest-free installments") under the buttons automatically. Turn it on in Settings > Payments under Shop Pay. There is no theme setting for it.

Gift cards

Gift card products work with the default product template. Dewline adds a "I want to send this as a gift" checkbox. When checked, shoppers can enter:

  • Recipient email
  • Recipient name (optional)
  • Message (optional)
  • Send on (optional), to schedule delivery

Shopify then emails the gift card to the recipient. Dynamic checkout buttons are hidden for gift cards so the recipient details are always collected. The gift card itself (the page your customer receives) uses the Dewline gift card template, with the code, balance, QR code and an Add to Apple Wallet link where available.

Below the product

The default product template also includes:

  • Product recommendations: "You may also like", based on Shopify's recommendations. Fine-tune them with the Search and Discovery app.
  • Recently viewed: products this shopper viewed before, stored in their browser. Hidden until they have viewed another product.

Tips

  • Use one product template for everything. Blocks with empty metafields hide themselves, so a serum will not show an empty supplement facts table.
  • Want a different layout for one product type? Select Create template from the page selector, then assign it in the product's Theme template setting.
