Collections, search and cart

This page covers how shoppers browse and buy: collection pages, filters, search, the cart drawer, the free shipping bar and product recommendations.

Collection pages

Collection pages use the Collection products section. In the theme editor, choose Collections > Default collection from the page selector, then select the section.

  • Show collection description and Show collection image
  • Products per page: 8 to 48
  • Columns on desktop, and 1 or 2 columns on phones
  • Load more: a "Load more" button instead of numbered pages
  • Enable filtering, Filter layout (sidebar or a bar above the grid) and Enable sorting

On phones, filters open in a drawer. Active filters show as chips that shoppers can remove one by one.

Product cards

Product cards appear on collections, search, featured collections and recommendations. Set them in Theme settings > Product cards:

  • Image shape: portrait, square or natural
  • Show second image on hover
  • Show vendor
  • Show product subtitle (from the descriptors.subtitle metafield)
  • Show star rating (from your product reviews app)
  • Show quick add: a plus button that adds the product without leaving the page. It shows on products with a single variant.
  • Tags shown as badges: product tags to show as badges, separated by commas, for example new, bestseller, vegan

Color swatches show under the price when a product's color option has swatches.

Set up filters

Dewline shows the filters you create in Shopify's free Search and Discovery app.

  1. Install Search and Discovery from the Shopify App Store.
  2. In the app, select Filters > Add filter.
  3. Choose a source, for example Availability, Price, Product type, Vendor, a Tag or a product metafield such as skin type or concern.
  4. Save. The filter appears on collection and search pages.

Price filters show as a range with minimum and maximum fields. Filters with color swatches show the swatches.

Tip: filter on metafields like "Skin type" or "Concern" so shoppers can narrow down by what matters most in beauty.

Search

  • Predictive search: as shoppers type, suggestions show matching products, collections, pages, articles and search terms. Turn it on or off in Theme settings > Search > Show suggestions as you type. You can also hide prices in the suggestions.
  • Search suggestions: in the Header section, add comma-separated suggestions (for example "vitamin C, SPF, retinol") to show when search opens.
  • Search results page: choose Search in the theme editor to set results per page, columns, load more, filters and sorting. Add Suggestions to show before a search and when nothing is found.

Cart drawer

In Theme settings > Cart:

  • Cart type: Drawer slides in from the side when a product is added. Page sends shoppers to the cart page.
  • Show order notes: a field for notes like gift messages.
  • Show currency codes: shows prices like "$25.00 USD". Useful if you sell in several currencies.

Free shipping bar

The cart drawer and cart page show a progress bar toward free shipping.

  1. Open Theme settings > Cart.
  2. In Free shipping threshold, enter an amount in your store currency, for example 50. Do not include a currency symbol.
  3. Leave it empty to hide the bar.

Shoppers see "You're $12.00 away from free shipping", then "You've unlocked free shipping".

Important: the bar only displays a message. Set up the free shipping rate itself in Settings > Shipping and delivery, using the same amount.

Note: the threshold is compared to the cart total in the shopper's currency. If you sell in several currencies with Shopify Markets, the bar uses the same number for every currency.

Recommendations

  • Cart drawer: turn on Show product recommendations in the cart drawer in Theme settings > Cart. Up to three "Pairs well with" products show, with an add button for single-variant products. Dewline shows complementary products first, then related products.
  • Product pages: the Product recommendations section shows "You may also like".

To choose the products yourself, open the Search and Discovery app, select Product recommendations, and set complementary and related products for each product.
