Translations and markets

Dewline's storefront text comes in English and French. Every button, label and message is included in both languages, so a French storefront needs no extra work for the theme's own text.

Change the theme's default text

You can reword any built-in text, like "Add routine to bag" or "Your bag".

  1. Go to Online Store > Themes.
  2. Next to Dewline, select the ... button, then Edit default theme content.
  3. Choose the language from the menu at the top.
  4. Search for the text you want to change, type your version and select Save.

Text you type into sections and blocks in the theme editor (headings, quiz questions, glossary entries) is your content, not theme text. Translate it as shown below.

Sell in French

  1. Go to Settings > Languages and select Add language. Choose French.
  2. Install the free Translate and Adapt app from Shopify to translate your own content: products, collections, pages, metafields and the text in your theme sections and blocks.
  3. When you are ready, select Publish next to French in Settings > Languages.

French shoppers then see the theme's French text and your translated content.

Add other languages

For a language other than English or French:

  1. Add and publish the language in Settings > Languages.
  2. Use Translate and Adapt to translate both the theme text and your content. The app can suggest automatic translations that you review.

Country and language selectors

When your store sells to more than one country or has more than one published language, Dewline shows selectors so shoppers can switch.

  • Footer: turn on Show country and language selectors in the Footer section settings.
  • Mobile menu: the selectors also show at the bottom of the menu drawer on phones and small screens.

The country selector shows each country with its currency, for example "Canada (CAD $)". A selector only appears if there is more than one choice.

Set up markets

  1. Go to Settings > Markets.
  2. Add the countries or regions you sell to and choose their currency.
  3. Turn on the languages for each market.

Prices on the storefront then show in the shopper's local currency. Turn on Theme settings > Cart > Show currency codes if your currencies share a symbol, like US and Canadian dollars.

Note: the free shipping bar threshold is a single number. With several currencies, it is compared against the cart total in the shopper's currency. See Collections, search and cart.
