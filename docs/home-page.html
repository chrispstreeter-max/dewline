Home page sections

Build your home page from sections. In the theme editor, open the home page and select Add section. Drag sections to reorder them. Every content section has a Color scheme and Top padding and Bottom padding settings (0 to 120 px).

Sections in the demo home page

The demo home page uses these sections, in this order:

  1. Hero
  2. Scrolling text
  3. Concern tiles
  4. Featured collection
  5. Routine builder
  6. Ingredient spotlight
  7. Results
  8. Image with text
  9. Testimonials
  10. Press logos
  11. FAQ

All sections

  • Hero: A large image with your headline and buttons. Choose Split (image beside text) or Overlay (text on the image), three heights and an optional phone image. Add Heading, Text, Buttons, Eyebrow and Concern links blocks inside it. An optional floating badge shows a number and a short line, for example "94%" and "saw brighter skin". Use only results you can support.
  • Scrolling text: A moving band of short phrases, separated by commas. Pick the size and speed. It stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
  • Concern tiles: Image tiles that link to collections by skin concern, like Dryness or Breakouts. Grid or side-scrolling, with optional product counts. Each tile uses the collection image and title unless you set your own.
  • Featured collection: A product grid or carousel. Add up to four collections to show them as tabs, or pick products by hand.
  • Collection list: Tiles for the collections you choose, or all collections. Grid or side-scrolling.
  • Routine builder: A morning and evening routine with a live total and "Add routine to bag". See Routines.
  • Ingredient spotlight: Cards for your hero ingredients with an image, concentration, role and a link. Link them to your ingredient glossary.
  • Results: Big numbers with labels, like "92% saw smoother skin", with an optional count-up animation. Add a footnote to say where the numbers come from. The demo numbers are examples: replace them with results you can support.
  • Before and after: A draggable comparison slider for two images, with labels and a caption. Side by side with text, or stacked.
  • Image with text: An image (or two overlapping images) beside your own blocks: heading, text, buttons and more.
  • Benefits: Icons with short titles and text, like "Fragrance-free" or "Vegan". Plain, panel or lined style.
  • Testimonials: Customer quotes with a name, a detail like skin type, a star rating and an optional product link.
  • Press logos: A row of logos from press or retailers. Optional grayscale until hover.
  • Video: An uploaded video or a YouTube or Vimeo link, with an optional cover image. Uploaded videos can autoplay muted while on screen.
  • Rich text: Centered or left-aligned text blocks: eyebrow, heading, text and buttons.
  • FAQ: Questions and answers in collapsible rows. Turn on structured data on one FAQ section per page to help search engines show your answers.
  • Newsletter: An email signup. Subscribers are added to your customer list with the tag "newsletter".
  • Custom Liquid and Apps: Your own Liquid code, or blocks from your installed apps.

Header and footer

The header and footer appear on every page. Select them in the theme editor sidebar.

  • Announcement bar: rotating messages with optional links above the header.
  • Header: your main menu, left or centered logo, sticky header, search and account icon. Customize search placeholder text and suggestions shown when search opens.
  • Footer: brand, menu and text columns, a newsletter signup, country and language selectors, payment icons, policy links and Follow on Shop. Social links come from Theme settings > Social media.

Tips

  • Keep the hero headline short. Six to eight words reads well on phones.
  • Link the hero button to your routine quiz. It is the fastest path to a full cart.
  • Use the dark color scheme (Scheme 3) for one section to break up a long page.
