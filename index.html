Signature features

Built for how people buy beauty

Shoppers want to know what is inside, what it does and what to use with it. Dewline answers all three on the page.

Routine quiz

Find your routine in 60 seconds

A step-by-step quiz built from theme editor blocks. Each answer scores your results, and the winning routine appears with its products and one button: Add my routine to bag. No app, no monthly fee.

  • Single or multiple choice questions with images
  • Weighted scoring you control, like dry:2, sensitive
  • Results with products, a collection link and an offer note
  • Remembers each shopper’s last result
Read the guide for routine quiz

Ingredients and facts

Product pages that explain themselves

Key ingredients, a results timeline, an accessible supplement facts panel, free-from badges, how to use and the full INCI list. Fill in a metafield once and every product shows its own data.

  • Connects to product metafields: no copy and paste
  • Blocks hide themselves when a product has no data
  • A to Z ingredient glossary with search and filters
  • Deep links like /pages/ingredients#niacinamide
Read the guide for ingredients and facts

Routine builder

Sell the routine, not just the product

Morning and evening routines on product pages and the home page. Shoppers uncheck what they own, watch the total update, then add the rest in one click. Subscribe and save shows automatically when you use a subscriptions app.

  • Morning and evening tabs with step labels
  • Live total and one-click add to bag
  • Product routines from list metafields
  • Selling plans with savings badge
